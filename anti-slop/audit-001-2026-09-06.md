## Direction

Reading this as: a local rain forecast for people in South Korea, in a chart-recorder visual language, ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.

Direction comes from ADR 0009 and ADR 0010, which record the owner's approved design. These dials interpret that direction; they do not introduce a new style.

- Color: blue identifies probability, teal identifies amount, amber identifies the rain window.
- Composition: the answer and timeline come first; comparison and scoring evidence sit on the receipts shelf.
- Typography: strong Korean reading text and compact numeric/meta text support a forecast instrument.
- Spacing: separate the answer from supporting evidence without making every section a bordered card.
- Motif: the repeated time grid represents the same forecast at different reading scales.
- Motion: the ribbon arrives once; the waiting instrument indicates an outstanding request; reduced motion disables animation.

The dark ground, graph motif, and quiet corners are documented choices. Replacing them with a generic light theme or removing the grid would conflict with the approved direction rather than improve originality.

## Findings

### 1. HIGH: Supporting text has insufficient contrast

Rules: R-25, C-4.
Reason: small labels and privacy/explanation text use a dim token that falls below 4.5:1 on their actual opaque backgrounds.

Browser measurements:

| Text | Foreground | Background | Size | Contrast |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chooser provider names | `#5e6c7b` | `#0b0f14` | 10.88px | 3.58:1 |
| Chooser privacy explanation | `#5e6c7b` | `#121820` | 9.92px | 3.32:1 |
| Record search label | `#5e6c7b` | `#121820` | 10.56px | 3.32:1 |
| Timeline time ranges on the plain ground | `#5e6c7b` | `#0b0f14` | 10.56px | 3.58:1 |

Source: `app/globals.css:49` (`--local-dim`), timeline ranges at `1270`, record label at `2574`. The same token also appears on record metadata and table headings.

Proposed fix: raise the dim text token enough for every surface on which it is used, and review hardcoded text colors separately. Preserve hierarchy through weight and spacing instead of reducing legibility. Verify computed contrast again, including translucent/sticky surfaces and waiting-state opacity.

### 2. HIGH: Several controls fall below the skill's 44px target

Rule: R-03.
Reason: essential navigation and density controls have small clickable rectangles, even though the surrounding layout fits the screen.

| Control | Measured clickable rectangle |
| --- | --- |
| Enabled chooser selection button | 34.95px wide, 44px high |
| Miniature timeline navigation | 22px high |
| Density toggle | 35.2px high |
| Forecast footer record link | 14px high |
| Record return link | 19.6px high |

Measured in Chromium at mobile widths. The 44px requirement is the selected skill's rule; this is not a claim that every listed link necessarily violates WCAG 2.2 AA target-size exceptions.

Source: `app/globals.css:1492` (`.local-minibar-spark`), `1535` (`.local-minibar-tgl`), `2532` (`.local-footer-link`), and the chooser search button styling.

Proposed fix: enlarge hit areas to at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels while keeping the visible graph and labels compact. Recheck the sticky bar at 320px so the larger targets do not collide or obscure content.

### 3. HIGH: Record search gives no feedback while a request is pending

Rules: R-27, C-4.
Reason: the record picker has empty/error messages but renders neither a loading indicator nor an accessible busy/status announcement.

With a controlled unresolved search request, the browser confirmed:

- The request had started.
- The picker contained only its question and the input.
- The input had no `aria-busy` value.
- The picker had no status/live region.
- Resolving the request to an empty result displayed the empty message, confirming that the request was active rather than absent.

Source: `components/local/RecordStationPicker.tsx:26` maps terminal outcomes to text but omits `searching`; rendering begins at `43`. The shared search hook already exposes that state.

Proposed fix: render a short loading message and accessible status feedback during search. Keep the existing distinctions among a rejected query, throttling, missing configuration, transient failure, and no matches. Announce those outcomes without moving keyboard focus.

### 4. HIGH: Visible text contains the punctuation prohibited by R-02

Rule: R-02.
Reason: user-facing titles, explanatory kickers, summary buttons, and missing-value markers contain U+2014.

Source examples:

- `app/layout.tsx:22`: document/social title.
- `app/behind-the-data/page.tsx:116`: explanation following the numbers-reading kicker; further examples occur throughout the record.
- `components/local/LocalForecastExperience.tsx:77`: absent probability marker, plus summary and rain-window text elsewhere in the file.
- `app/opengraph-image.tsx:3`: image alternative text.

This is a selected-skill compliance finding, not evidence that this punctuation harms comprehension. It is lower practical urgency than findings 1 through 3, despite the required HIGH tier.

Proposed fix: replace prose separators with a colon, comma, or parentheses. Replace absent-data glyphs with concise Korean absence labels where appropriate; preserve the distinction between unpublished data and a published zero. Update affected text expectations in tests. Do not mechanically rewrite historical ADRs or code comments as part of a UI punctuation fix.

# The page after the density toggle was removed

Date: 2026-10-13 · antislop after mode, selected by the owner · report only · release candidate 2.2.4 (`5d72ecd`)

No application code was changed by this audit. Findings are numbered; nothing is fixed until the owner approves specific numbers.

## What was checked

- Page: forecast (`/`) dashboard for 서울특별시 중구 명동, live provider data, a rain-expected day.
- Engine: Chromium (Playwright), production build of `5d72ecd` served locally. Production had not deployed this commit when the audit ran (Vercel incident), so nothing here was observed on the live site.
- Widths: 390, 768 and 1440px, at the top of the page and scrolled so the mini-ribbon is pinned.
- Scope: the pinned mini-ribbon and the sections the fold used to hide. The rest of the page was audited in audit 007 and not rechecked.

## What holds

- No sideways page scroll at any of the three widths.
- The mini-ribbon keeps a 60px height and its one control (the jump to the timeline) keeps a 44px-tall target.
- The mini-ribbon still pins: at 9px from the top at 768 and 1440px, and directly beneath the location strip at 390px.
- The comparison, outlook and record sections render in full on a first visit, on the receipts shelf, with their kickers.
- On desktop the summary sentence sits flush with the right edge of the bar; the slot the button left is not an empty gap.

## Findings

### 1. At the top of the page the mini-ribbon repeats what is directly above and below it (MEDIUM, C-3, R-31)

At 1440px the bar's sentence is the headline again, word for word ("지금부터 오후 4시까지 비 예상"), followed by "최대 86%", which the line under the headline also states. Its bars are a miniature of the timeline that starts about 70px below it. The bar exists for the scrolled state, where the headline and timeline are off screen; at rest it is a third statement of the same verdict. The toggle used to give the bar a second job at rest, and that job is gone.

At 390px the sentence is hidden, so the bar at rest is "24H" and eight unlabeled bars above the labeled timeline.

Options: show the bar only once the timeline has scrolled out of view; or keep it in place and hide its sentence until it is pinned.

### 2. A production build run outside Vercel logs two console errors (MEDIUM, R-35)

`npm start` on a local build requests `/_vercel/insights/script.js`, which only Vercel serves: one 404 and one "Refused to execute script" error per page load. This comes from release 2.2.3 (analytics), not from the toggle removal. `docs/VERIFYING.md` expects a clean console in browser checks, and the Playwright suite runs against this same local server. Production is not affected: the script answered 200 there.

Option: render the analytics component only when the build runs on Vercel.

### 3. The mini-ribbon marks the rain window by brightness, the timeline marks it in amber (LOW, R-31)

ADR 0009 gives amber to the rain window. In the timeline the rain-window blocks are amber; in the mini-ribbon the same blocks are full-strength blue and the rest are half-strength blue. Both are readable alone, but with the bar now a plainer element directly above the timeline, the two charts disagree about what marks the window. This predates the removal.

Option: colour the mini-ribbon's rain-window blocks amber.

## Not findings

- The longer first visit (document height 3,653px at 390px) is the accepted cost recorded in `docs/DECISIONS.md`, 2026-10-13.
- The README screenshot still showed the button when the audit ran. It was retaken from production in 2.2.5.

## Follow-up, 2026-10-13: layout sweep after the release

The owner asked for a check that removing the button left no alignment, spacing or line-break defect in any scenario. 2.2.4 was live by then (production served `5d72ecd`, and the live page had no button and no fold). No findings were approved and no application code changed.

Method: the 2.2.4 production build served locally with controlled forecast responses. Eight states (rain at midday, rain running into tomorrow, rain for the whole window, open-ended rain, two separate rain runs, dry, a partial response, nothing published) × 16 widths (320, 360, 390, 430, 540, 640, 641, 768, 820, 900, 901, 1024, 1180, 1280, 1440, 1920px) × root text at 100, 130 and 200%, each at the top of the page and with the mini-ribbon pinned. Chromium and WebKit, 384 measured layouts each. For local WebKit only, the content security policy header was removed from the document, because its `upgrade-insecure-requests` cannot work on a local http server.

Result for the mini-ribbon, identical in both engines:

- No sideways page scroll, and no child of the bar outside the bar, in any layout.
- The label, the bars and the sentence share one vertical centre; left and right padding are equal.
- At 100% text the bar is 60px tall with 8px above and 12px below, in every state and width. At 130% it is 65px.
- The sentence stays on one line at 100% and 130%.
- At 200% text the sentence moves to its own row beneath the bars at 641–901px (two lines at 641px, breaking between "…까지" and "비 예상"), and at 320px the "24H" label and the bars take separate rows. Nothing is clipped. The removed button wrapped the same way at this size, so this is not new.

The gap measurements at 130% and 200% were taken with the page scrolled and are not reliable; only the 100% gaps are reported.

Limits: controlled responses are layout evidence, not live weather. Real devices were not used.

## Follow-up, 2026-10-13: findings 1–3 fixed (2.2.6)

The owner approved "1A, 2 fix, 3 amber".

1. The mini-ribbon is hidden at rest and appears once the timeline has scrolled off the top. It is an overlay on a zero-height sticky anchor, so nothing moves when it appears.
2. Analytics renders only when the build runs on Vercel. A local production build no longer requests `/_vercel/insights/script.js`.
3. The mini-ribbon's rain-window bars are amber.

Checked: three new browser tests (hidden at rest, visible after scrolling, layout unmoved, amber bars; no analytics request off Vercel) failed on 2.2.5 and pass now. Measured in Chromium and WebKit at 320, 390, 640, 641, 768, 1024, 1440 and 1920px with 100, 130 and 200% text: hidden at rest everywhere, no sideways scroll, pinned height 60px (100%) and 65px (130%), pinned directly beneath the location strip on phones.

Limits: where the two pinned bars would exceed 30% of the screen (200% text up to 900px) the bar stays hidden. Controlled responses, no real devices.

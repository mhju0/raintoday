# UI audit 003: Korean line breaks and punctuation on iPhone widths

Date: 2026-09-25
Mode: AFTER. The antislop skill was not loaded in this session, so the audit applies the ADR 0009/0010 direction and the owner's stated rules directly.
Scope: the working tree of `fix/chooser-first-screen` on top of `f758878`, as a production build.
Status: the owner approved all five findings, and all five are fixed on the same branch.

Follow-up: after the fixes, the same audit reports 0 findings at all 10 widths in both engines. The fixes:

- Headline commas are gone, and each clause is a unit that sits on its own line at 640px and below.
- The three headings lose their periods.
- Quoted and bracketed terms and 오늘·내일 are bound with `.local-keep`. This includes spots the audit had not yet caught:
  - ‘누락’ and ‘오보’
  - the footer and chooser 오늘·내일
  - the search notice's ‘내 위치로 보기’
- One pre-existing oddity is still open and outside this audit: a rain window can read "모두 0mm" when its blocks all publish 0 mm.

## Owner's rules

- A heading does not end in a period, and when its clauses fall on separate lines it does not need commas at the line ends.
- A Korean word never breaks across lines. That includes a quote mark or bracket left behind at a line end.
- Covers the iPhone SE 1st gen through the Pro Max sizes, in Safari and Chrome, single screen only.

## Method

- Engines: Playwright WebKit (Safari's engine) and Chromium in mobile emulation, with the page's own web fonts loaded.
- Widths (CSS px):
  - 320 (SE 1)
  - 375 (SE 2/3, 6–8, X/XS/11 Pro, 12/13 mini)
  - 390, 393, 402, 414
  - 420 (Air)
  - 428, 430, 440 (16/17 Pro Max)
  - The 18 Pro Max is unannounced, so 440 stands in for it.
- Views:
  - the home screen (chooser)
  - the loading overlay
  - the forecast in both densities, for four live locations: 보라동, 해운대 우동, 제주 연동, 대관령면
  - `/behind-the-data`
- Checks run on every visible text node:
  - **Split words:** each Hangul token's characters are measured, and a token that spans two line boxes is a split.
  - **Heading punctuation:** headings, labels and buttons are checked for a period or comma that ends a line.
  - **Cut-off content:** any element past the viewport edge that no clipping ancestor contains.
- Limits:
  - Forecast copy depends on live data, so dry-day and error sentences were only exercised where the data produced them.
  - Emulation is not a device. The iOS input zoom, for example, cannot be reproduced this way.

## Clean results

- **Home screen and loading overlay:** no findings at any width in either engine.
- **Cut-off content:** none in any view at any width. The cropped charts reported on an iPhone 13 mini are therefore not a layout overflow. They match iOS Safari's focus zoom on a search input under 16px, which stays after the forecast loads. The fix for that is in this branch, but the device check is still pending.

## Findings

### 1. HIGH: the forecast headline ends lines in commas

Where: the forecast `h1`, built by `RainSentence` in `components/local/LocalForecastExperience.tsx`. It appears at every width, in both engines, for every rainy location.

Example: "비 예상: 지금부터, / 내일 새벽 4시까지, / 모두 2.3mm".

Proposal: drop both separator commas. On narrow screens, set each clause (start, end, total) as its own line, so the line break does the separating.

### 2. MEDIUM: the `/behind-the-data` claim headings end in a period

Where: the three `h3` elements at `app/behind-the-data/page.tsx:119`, `:126` and `:133`. They appear at every width.

Proposal: remove the trailing period. The wording stays the same.

### 3. MEDIUM: Safari leaves an opening quote at the end of a line

Where: the seed method note on the forecast in full view. It appears in WebKit only, at 402–440.

Examples: "‘ / 비를 놓친 날’" and "‘ / 헛예보’는".

Proposal: bind each quoted term into one unbreakable unit. Terms only, never whole sentences.

### 4. LOW: Safari leaves an opening bracket at the end of a line

Where: "Brier (낮을수록 좋음)" in the forecast's full-view benchmark line. It appears in WebKit only, at 320.

Proposal: bind "(낮을수록 좋음)" the same way as finding 3.

### 5. LOW: "오늘·내일" splits at the middle dot

Where: a `/behind-the-data` paragraph. It appears at 440 in both engines.

Proposal: keep "오늘·내일" unbreakable wherever it appears.

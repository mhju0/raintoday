# Service-wide layout and typography audit

Date: 2026-10-11 · antislop after mode, selected by the owner · report only · release 2.1.3 (`487dc2a`)

No application code was changed. Findings are numbered; nothing is fixed until the owner approves specific numbers.

Follow-up: the owner approved findings 1–7. They are fixed in release 2.1.4; see [Follow-up](#follow-up-findings-17-fixed-in-214) at the end. The owner then approved findings 8–12; they are fixed in release 2.1.5, see [the second follow-up](#follow-up-findings-812-fixed-in-215).

## What was checked

- Pages: forecast (`/`: chooser, loading, dashboard, both error cards), scoring record (`/behind-the-data`, with and without a station, with the picker open) and the 404 page.
- Engines: Chromium and WebKit (Playwright 1.63), production build served locally.
- Widths: 320, 360, 390, 430, 540, 640, 641, 768, 820, 900, 901, 1024, 1180, 1280, 1440 and 1920px.
- Text size: 100%, 130% and 200% root size.
- Forecast states (controlled responses): dry, dry with trace rain, mixed, mixed without amounts, all wet, rain tomorrow, two rain runs, rain running past the window, tiny total, fully unpublished, partly unpublished, three blocks only, day total only, no timeline, 0%/100% extremes with a long place name and sub-zero temperature, learned weights, failing benchmark, seed evidence, empty detail and device location.
- Per page, an in-page detector measured line breaks, short last lines, clipped or truncated text, overlap, baseline and edge alignment within rows, chart origins and tap-target size. 905 measured page states per engine.

## What holds

At 100% text, in both engines, at every width:

- No sideways page scroll, no element outside the viewport, no clipped or truncated text, no console errors.
- No Korean word broken mid-word and no line starting with a closing punctuation mark.
- **The 2.1.3 baseline fix holds.** Across all 20 forecast states, the eight ribbon tracks share one top and one bottom, bars share one zero line, and the 40% line, the mm lane, the axis ticks and the sticky-bar sparkline have 0px spread.

The class of defect that prompted this audit (a longer text moving a chart off a shared axis) survives in three smaller places: findings 4, 5 and 7.

## Findings

### 1. On phones the two sticky bars sit on top of each other

Priority: HIGH · R-03 (mobile layout), layoutmobile "Sticky Nav Steals the Screen"

At 640px and below the location strip sticks to the top (`app/globals.css:2237-2245`, `top: 0`, `z-index: 2`). The 24-hour summary bar also sticks (`app/globals.css:1594`, `top: 0.55rem`, `z-index: 40`). Once the reader scrolls past the chart, the summary bar covers the strip.

- 390px, 서울특별시: strip 0–74px, summary bar 9–69px. The place name, temperature and `위치 바꾸기` are hidden; only a 5px sliver of the strip shows.
- 320px, or any long place name: strip 0–106px; the lower half of its second row shows beneath the bar as cut-off text.

The strip's own comment calls it "the only navigation" on the phone page, and it is unreachable for most of the scroll. Both engines.

Evidence: [390px](evidence-007-2026-10-11/01-sticky-390.png).
Suggested fix: stack them (give the summary bar a `top` equal to the strip's height on phones) or let only one stick.

### 2. The chart's hover/keyboard readout is cut off at the edges

Priority: HIGH · R-03, layoutmobile "Overflow Hidden Clipping"

The readout is centred on the column with `white-space: nowrap` (`app/globals.css:1333-1348`) inside a scroller that clips. On the last column it is cut on the right at every width including 1440px (`강수 0.7mm` loses its end). On the first column it is cut on the left at 768px and below. It also covers the period label of the column it describes.

On phones, arrow keys move the selection to columns that are scrolled out of view; the chart does not scroll to follow.

Evidence: [1440px, last column](evidence-007-2026-10-11/03-scrub-readout-1440.png).
Suggested fix: clamp the readout's position inside the scroller, and scroll the selected column into view.

### 3. Search messages cover the shortcut chips

Priority: MEDIUM · C-4 (resilience in error states)

"No match", "search unavailable", "search not available here" and "location denied" messages render in the search popup (`app/globals.css:541-548`). The popup is out of flow on purpose, so the page does not jump while typing. For a result list that is right. For a one-line message it hides the `바로 보기` label and slices the 서울/부산/제주/강릉 chips in half, which are the one way forward when search has failed. The popup is also darker (`--local-bg`) than the panel it sits in. All 16 widths, both engines.

Evidence: [390px](evidence-007-2026-10-11/02-chooser-message-390.png).
Suggested fix: keep results in the popup; render messages in flow, or reserve their height.

### 4. A column with no published value starts its caption higher

Priority: MEDIUM · R-03, C-4

`미발표` is set smaller than the numerals (`app/globals.css:1840-1843` against `1488`). The caption below it therefore starts higher than in the other columns: 2px at 1024px, 5.8px at 1180px, 8.2px at 1280px and 9.8px at 1440px and above. The bars are unaffected; the row of captions is not level.

Evidence: [1440px](evidence-007-2026-10-11/04-unpublished-caption-1440.png).
Suggested fix: give the value line a fixed height so the caption row has one origin.

### 5. Provider comparison bars are drawn on different scales in narrow cards

Priority: MEDIUM · C-4

When the card is under 18rem wide, each row sizes its own columns by content (`app/globals.css:2083-2086`). A row reading `100%` or `미발표` gets a shorter track than a row reading `80%`. Measured at 320, 360 and 641px: the 100% track is 125.5px, the others 133.2px (7.7px apart; 10px at 130% text, 15px at 200%). The bars are meant to be compared with each other, so they need one scale. The percentage column's right edge shifts by the same amount.

Evidence: [320px](evidence-007-2026-10-11/05-06-meters-outlook-320.png).
Suggested fix: fixed widths for the value and weight columns in the narrow layout, as in the wide one.

### 6. The six-day outlook leaves one day alone on a second row

Priority: MEDIUM · R-03, owner rule "nothing left alone at a line end"

`auto-fit` with a 2.5rem minimum (`app/globals.css:2095-2099`) fits five columns at 320px and 641px, so the sixth day drops to its own row. At 130% text it also happens at 390 and 768px.

Evidence: [320px](evidence-007-2026-10-11/05-06-meters-outlook-320.png).
Suggested fix: explicit column counts (6, or 3+3) instead of `auto-fit`.

### 7. Today and tomorrow cards do not line up row for row

Priority: MEDIUM · R-05 (rhythm), C-4

Tomorrow's amount carries a range note (`많으면 … · 적으면 …`) that today's does not. Side by side (641px and up):

- 641–1023px: tomorrow's amount drops below the percentage while today's stays beside it. The temperature rows sit 66px apart.
- 1024px and up: the note wraps to two lines; temperature and explanation rows sit 18.7px apart.

The note's second line also starts with the separator: `· 적으면 0mm (기상청)`.

Evidence: [768px](evidence-007-2026-10-11/07-day-cards-768.png), [1440px](evidence-007-2026-10-11/07-day-cards-1440.png).
Suggested fix: share rows between the two cards (subgrid, as the ribbon now does), and break the note before `적으면` without a leading dot.

### 8. Record page: the timestamp wraps and pushes its label up

Priority: LOW · R-05

In the summary box (`app/globals.css:2500-2514`) at 768px and up, `2026년 10월 11일 오후 8:15` wraps as `2026년 10월 ⏎ 11일 오후 8:15`, splitting the date. Its label then sits 13px higher than the other three.

Separately: the same code printed `PM 8:15` on this machine (Node 24.15, ICU 78.2) and `오후 8:24` on production. `app/behind-the-data/page.tsx:21-27` relies on the runtime's locale data, so a runtime update on the host could switch production to `PM`.

Evidence: [1440px](evidence-007-2026-10-11/08-record-meta-1440.png).
Suggested fix: align items to the start, keep date and time on separate planned lines, and build the `오전/오후` string explicitly.

### 9. The 404 page is a different colour and its link does not look like one

Priority: LOW · R-03 (tap target), R-26

`app/not-found.tsx:5` uses `.btd` without the `.btd-page` wrapper, so the page shows the body colour `rgb(4, 6, 13)`, not the site ground `#0b0f14`. The stylesheet comment at `app/globals.css:2421-2422` names this exact effect as the reason the wrapper exists. `예보로 돌아가기` (`app/not-found.tsx:12`) is ink-coloured, not underlined and 107×21px, so it reads as a sentence and is under the 44px touch height.

Evidence: [390px](evidence-007-2026-10-11/09-not-found-390.png).

### 10. Small alignment and spacing items

Priority: LOW · R-05, R-06

- a. The mm-lane values (`0`, `미발표`) sit flush to the column edge; the labels and percentages above are inset 4px (`app/globals.css:1525`). Visible in [finding 4's image](evidence-007-2026-10-11/04-unpublished-caption-1440.png).
- b. Ribbon head: the source chip and `3시간 블록 8개` sit on baselines 4px apart at 430px and up (`app/globals.css:1236-1250`; the chip is centred, the row is baseline-aligned).
- c. Chooser `다시 시도` button is 49×36px, under the 44px touch height.
- d. Record picker: the paragraph after the open picker starts with no gap below the picker's border.
- e. Two-line headings are tight: the evidence heading at line-height 1.04, the chooser headline at 1.08, the forecast answer at 1.10 on 320–390px. Hangul has no descenders to spare, so stacked lines nearly touch.
- f. At desktop width, the action sentence is balanced into `…이후 내일 ⏎ 아침 6시부터…`, splitting a time phrase.

### 11. Korean labels use a different typeface from Korean body text

Priority: LOW · R-06 · owner decision, not a defect to fix blind

Labels, chips, tags and units use the mono stack (`app/globals.css:61`), which has no Hangul. Chromium on macOS reports `SF Mono` for Latin and `Apple SD Gothic Neo` for Hangul in those elements, while body text is Noto Sans KR. Windows and Android will substitute their own system Hangul. Word spaces in those labels are mono-width, so `5개  서비스  동일  비중` reads loosely spaced. Verified on macOS Chromium only.

Suggested fix, if wanted: add Noto Sans KR after the mono families in `--local-mono`.

### 12. Enlarged text only (130% and 200%)

Priority: LOW · C-4

- WebKit, 130%, 320/390px: the opening quote is left alone at a line end (`‘ ⏎ 내 위치로 보기’`) in the "search not available" message.
- WebKit, 130%, 1024px: `기상청 ASOS ⏎ · 익일 예보만`, a line starting with the separator.
- WebKit, 200%: `5 ⏎ km`; ribbon range labels overlap at 320/390/768px.
- Both engines, 200%: words break mid-word (`최 ⏎ 근`, `관측 ⏎ 소로`, `평균했습니 ⏎ 다.`); ribbon captions overlap at 1024px.
- Chromium, 130%, 320/390px: short last lines in the record page's notes.

## Checked and not a defect

- The loading overlay over the dimmed chooser: legible, intended.
- The two evidence cards' explanation paragraphs starting at different heights: different content above them.
- Result lists covering the chips while typing: intended popup behaviour (finding 3 is about messages only).
- Detector hits for `이후 ⏎ 5–60%로`, `성능 반영 ⏎ 0.080`, `5개 서비스 ⏎ 동일 비중`: breaks at real spaces, misread by the detector.

## Limits

- Layout evidence from controlled responses, not live weather. One live check: the production record page's timestamp text.
- Chromium and WebKit desktop builds at phone widths, not real devices. No Firefox. No real iOS Safari or Android Chrome.
- Finding 11's font report is macOS Chromium only.
- WebKit needed the local server's CSP and HSTS headers removed to load assets over plain http; this is a test-harness detail, not a product issue.
- Light mode, print and right-to-left were not checked (the site ships one dark theme).

## Follow-up: findings 1–7 fixed in 2.1.4

| # | Change |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | On phones the summary bar sticks beneath the location strip. The strip publishes its height. If the two together exceed 30% of the screen height (enlarged text on a small phone), the summary scrolls away and the strip alone stays. |
| 2 | The readout stays centred on its block but is held inside the visible chart. A block reached by arrow key is scrolled into view. |
| 3 | Search messages render in the flow, above the shortcut chips. The result list and the "searching" line stay in the popup. The message's room is held while the field has text, so the page is resized once, not per keystroke. |
| 4 | `미발표` keeps its smaller type in the same line box as the numerals. |
| 5 | Narrow provider rows use fixed value columns, so every track is the same length. |
| 6 | The outlook has an explicit column count: one row, or two even rows in a narrow card (two columns at extreme text sizes). |
| 7 | The two day cards share their four rows. Each separator in the amount note sits with the phrase before it. |

Verification: 21 new browser tests. Twenty fail on 2.1.3 and pass on 2.1.4; the keystroke-resize test for finding 3 passes on both and guards the existing behaviour. Full browser suite (62 tests, Chromium), lint, typecheck, unit and route tests and build pass. The 905-state matrix was re-run in Chromium and WebKit: readout and off-screen-column detections went from 48 to 0, and no new clipping, overflow or line-break detections appeared.

Known trade-offs:

- Shared rows leave empty space in the shorter day card at 641–1023px, where tomorrow's amount note takes three lines.
- At 320px and 641px the outlook is two rows of three.
- A line of the amount note can end with `·`.
- The first search message moves the content below it down by two lines.
